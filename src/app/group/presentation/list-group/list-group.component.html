Create a group and add members to participate in a consensus workshop. The goal is to reach a mutually agreed-upon research topic that aligns with the interests of all team members. 🎯📚

Before creating a group, build its seed team: add up to 5 researchers from the corpus and Centinela suggests collaborators by topical affinity, re-ranked for institutional diversity and emerging profiles. Candidates update automatically as the team changes; invite the ones who are not on Centinela yet.
